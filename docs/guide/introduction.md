# Introduction

StellarSplit is a modern, AI-powered expense management platform built on the Stellar network. It combines the speed and efficiency of Soroban smart contracts with the intelligence of Google Gemini to provide a seamless financial experience.

## The Vision

Managing shared expenses (like dinner with friends or group trips) often involves tedious manual entry and chasing payments. StellarSplit simplifies this by:

1.  **AI Natural Language Processing**: Just tell the assistant what you spent, and it handles the rest.
2.  **Social Trust (Karma)**: A reputation system that incentivizes prompt payments.
3.  **Cross-Chain Interoperability**: Seamlessly move funds between Stellar and other ecosystems.

## Core Pillars

- **Trust**: Verified on-chain transactions.
- **Simplicity**: No complex crypto jargon; just chat with the AI.
- **Interoperability**: Connect with the wider DeFi world via Wormhole.
